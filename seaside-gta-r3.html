Seaside summer

Steal cars, run jobs from the pink phone marker, dodge the cops and earn cash for a speedboat. The map now stretches from the old town to Downtown and the port (press M). Out in the bay: sail with the wind, fish, race the regatta and hit the stunt ramps. WASD or arrows to move, E to enter, exit and buy, Space for the handbrake, Shift to sprint, R for the radio, H for the horn.

On the radar: yellow is your objective, pink is the jobs phone, green is the spray shop (repairs the car and clears your wanted level for $100). Progress saves in this browser.

  • Leave the boat away from the pier and you dive in and swim. Walk into the sea to swim, swim to shore to walk again.

Screen and radar

  • Top right: clock, cash, red health bar, orange car-damage bar (in a car), five wanted stars.
  • Top left: what you're driving, the radio station, and the job timer while on a job.
  • Bottom: the current objective in yellow, hints and messages above it. A yellow arrow at the screen edge points to the objective.
  • In a boat the top-left panel adds speed in knots, the wind direction with an arrow showing where it blows to, sail power (sailboat) and your fish hold.
  • Radar (bottom left) follows you and points your arrow the way you're facing; far-away objectives, the nearest jobs phone, your car and the police stick to its edge. The district name appears bottom right when you cross into a new area.
  • Radar blips: yellow = objective, pink = jobs phone, green = spray shop, orange = your own car, brown / white / teal = boat, sailboat, jet ski, flashing red/blue = police, red dot = you. On the water also: blue = regatta start, pale dots = fishing shoals, red dots = stunt ramps.

Jobs (pink marker by the phone booth)

  • Walk or drive into a pink ring to start the next job: there are phone booths in the old town, in the downtown park and at the port. Seven jobs rotate; each round pays more and the clock gets tighter.
  • Special delivery: pick up a package by car and deliver it in time.
  • Coast road sprint: hit nine checkpoints across both districts.
  • Hot wheels: steal the gold Coupe GT (instant 2 stars), stop it in the garage marker on the north road. Less damage = more pay.
  • Smuggler's run: fish three crates out of the bay by boat, bring them to the pier.
  • Island drop: boat to the island, grab the briefcase on foot (harbour police come after you), bring it back to the phone.
  • Cross-town courier: three drops spread over the whole map. Use the ring roads.
  • Port run: pick up the gold van at the port (1 star) and drive it to downtown. It's slow, and damage costs you money.
  • A job fails if the timer runs out, you get wasted or busted, or the job car is wrecked.
  • Rank by jobs passed: Tourist, Local (2), Hustler (5), Wheelman (9), Kingpin of the coast (14).

Boats and the sea

  • The fleet: wooden boat at the pier (becomes the speedboat once bought), sailboat moored on the south side of the pier, jet ski on the east shore of the big island (fastest, tightest turning). Board whichever is closest with E; job markers that need a boat accept any of them.
  • Wind shifts slowly during the day. The sailboat is silent and free to run but needs the wind: watch the arrow and the sail % in the panel.
  • Fishing: look for ripples with circling gulls. Stop the boat on the shoal and wait for the bar to fill. The hold takes 6 fish: sardine $15, mackerel $25, sea bream $45, sea bass $70, tuna $150, swordfish $300 (rare). A shoal moves after 2–3 catches.
  • Fish market: bring the boat to the pier and slow down: the whole catch sells automatically.
  • Bay regatta: drive a boat into the blue GO ring north-west of the pier (no job running, no wanted level). Pass all 8 gates between the orange buoys, out around the lighthouse and back. Finishing pays $120, a new personal best pays $300. Best time is saved. Try it under sail.
  • Stunt ramps: three red ramps in the bay. Hit one in the direction of the arrows at speed and you fly; longer jumps pay more and your record is saved. No steering in the air.
  • Places: the lighthouse rock far to the north-west (beam sweeps at night, gems on the rock), a palm islet to the south-west with shells and gems, and grey rocks that stop boats dead. You can land and walk on all of them.
  • Sea life: other sailboats cruise their own routes, and the dolphin pod comes to ride your bow wave when you go fast nearby.
  • Wake foam, night running light, and police boats at 2+ stars work everywhere on the bigger sea. Stars and pearls are scattered all over it.

Taxi fares

  • Steal a yellow taxi with no job running and no wanted level: fares start by themselves.
  • Stop in the marker next to the waving passenger, then stop in the drop-off marker before the timer ends.
  • Longer trips pay more, and every fifth fare in a row adds a $100 bonus. Leaving the taxi ends the shift.

Wanted level and police

  • Stars go up for: carjacking when police are near (or a witness calls it in), stealing a police cruiser, knocking over pedestrians, ramming police cars.
  • More stars = more and faster cruisers. At 2+ stars police boats chase you on the water too.
  • Busted: stopping, or being pinned, with police right next to you for about a second. A warning shows first, so keep moving.
  • Lose them: get well away from every cop until the stars flash and clear, or use the spray shop.
  • Spray shops (green markers: west side of Eastside, and the west road of Downtown): stop in it with a car, $100, full repair, new colour, wanted level cleared. It refuses while a cop is close by.

Health, damage, wasted and busted

  • Moving cars hurt you on foot. Health slowly regenerates while you're not wanted.
  • Crashes damage the car. Below 40% it smokes, below 30% it slows down, at zero it burns for 3 seconds and explodes: bail out!
  • Wasted = wake up at the nearest hospital. Busted = released at the nearest police station (one of each north-east of the old town and in Downtown). Either costs up to $100 and clears your wanted level.
  • Your own orange car returns to the driveway by the beach if you abandon it far away, and is replaced there if it gets destroyed.

Traffic and pedestrians

  • Sedan (all-rounder), Coupe GT (fast, fragile), Van (slow, tough), Taxi (enables fares), Police cruiser (fast, tough, 2 stars for stealing one).
  • Traffic brakes for you, honks when blocked, and eventually shoves abandoned cars to the kerb. Driving off-road is much slower.
  • Pedestrians jump out of the way of fast cars. Knocked-over ones drop cash, then get up and run.

Money

  • Earn: jobs, taxi fares, fishing, the regatta, stunt jumps, dropped cash, coins $5 (land), shells $5 (beach, on foot), stars $10 (by boat), pearls $15 (swimming), gems $25 (land and island). Everything respawns.
  • Parking challenge: reverse into the yellow P slot in the lay-by and hold still: $10 parked, $20 good, $30 perfect.
  • Spend (white $ markers, on foot, press E): speedboat $2,000 next to the pier, Coupe GT as your driveway car $4,000 north of the driveway.
  • Cash, rank, fares and purchases are saved in this browser.

Summer

  • Beach life: umbrellas, towels, sunbathers, sandcastles and beach balls along the town beach and on the islands, a lifeguard tower, a beach volleyball match, swimmers and inflatables (find the flamingos) in the shallows, windsurfers out in the bay, sun glitter on the water, flowers and extra palms in town. People wear summer colours and straw hats.
  • Ice cream van: one is parked by the beach south of the phone booth, and more drive around. Get in and the jingle starts (the radio ducks). With no job and no wanted level you get ice cream rounds: drive to the waving crowd, stop in the marker, get paid. Every 5 stops in a row adds $80.
  • Ice cream kiosks (pink rings: town beach near the volleyball court, the downtown park, the big island): on foot, E, $10, restores most of your health.
  • Sunset Beach FM: a new tropical station, and it's the one playing when you start.
  • Long days: a warm golden light by day, a long pink sunset around 20:00, short nights. Gulls cry near the water by day, crickets chirp inland at night.
  • Summer nights: fireworks over the bay from 22:00 to 23:30, and a bonfire beach party on the big island after dark.

World

  • A full day takes 12 minutes. At night cars use headlights, street lamps and windows light up, and the lighthouse sweeps the sea.
  • Old town (where you start): pier, your driveway, the first phone booth, parking lay-by, garage, with police and hospital just to the north-east.
  • Eastside: the second loop south-east of the old town, with the first spray shop.
  • Downtown: a 2×2 grid of tower blocks far to the east, reached by the highway from the old town's east road or the link from Eastside. City park with a phone booth, second hospital, police station and spray shop.
  • The port: south along the coast road. Quay with a crane, a second pier with another boat (you can dock and sell fish there too), warehouses, container stacks and a phone booth.
  • Ring roads: highway old town to Downtown, Downtown south and back west to the port, port north to the old town, plus a link from Eastside to the port. Traffic drives all of them; off-road across the fields is slow.
  • Countryside: fields and trees between the districts. Few people, coins and gems lying around.
  • The sea now runs the full height of the map: the bay and lighthouse in the north, and a south sea with three more islets, more rocks, two more stunt ramps, more shoals and another sailing route.
  • Traffic and pedestrians appear around wherever you are, so every district feels alive.

Cheats

  • Just type them while playing: CASH +$500, NOCOPS clears the wanted level, FIXIT repairs the car and heals you.
