Ready to catch?

Slide the bucket with your mouse, your finger, or the arrow keys. Catch 5 in a row for double points!

    What's new in this version

    • Streaks: catch 5 in a row and every pencil is worth double. A counter shows how many you have in a row.
    • Levels: fill the bar at the top to level up. Pencils fall faster each level, and the paper changes from notebook to chalkboard, blueprint, art room and outer space.
    • Power-ups in bubbles: a heart gives a life back, the magnet pulls pencils to you, the clock slows everything down, and the arrows make your bucket giant.
    • Rainbow pencil: catch it to start a pencil storm.
    • Golden pencil: worth 5 points, but it falls fast.
    • Ink blob: a grumpy blob to dodge. Catching it costs a life.
    • New buckets: unlock the Cat, Robot, Dragon, Rainbow and King buckets at 40, 120, 250, 500 and 1000 pencils caught.
    • Sound and pause: sound effects with an on/off button, and a pause button (or press P).
    • More ways to play: mouse, finger, arrow keys, or A and D. Press space to start.

    Keys: arrows or A and D to move, P to pause, space to play. Every pencil you catch counts toward new buckets, even after the game ends. Your best score and buckets are saved on this device.
