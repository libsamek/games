Baltimore free roam · The Wire edition

A fan-made fork of the seaside game, moved to Baltimore: the towers and the Pit, the container terminal, Hamsterdam, the middle school, the newsroom. Five phones hand out work for five institutions. North points right, the harbor is on the left; press M for the map. WASD or arrows to move, E to enter, exit and buy, Space for the handbrake, Shift to sprint, R radio, H horn.

On the radar: yellow is your objective, pink a job phone, green a spray shop, orange ring a carry-out. Inside the red dashed line of Hamsterdam the police leave you alone.

The map

  • Baltimore is drawn with north pointing right, so the harbor (south) is on the left of the screen, West Baltimore is at the top and East Baltimore at the bottom. Press M for the full map.
  • It's a compressed, inspired-by layout, not a street atlas: real neighborhoods and landmarks in roughly the right relation to each other, at game scale.

The cast

  • Every job phone has a named contact standing next to it (a top-down figure with a name tag), and each gives you a briefing and a reaction when you pass or fail. Almost all lines are newly written for this game in the characters' voices.
  • Quoted from the show (short signature lines, used where they fit): Lester's "All the pieces matter." when The tail succeeds, D'Angelo's "The king stay the king." after the Re-up round, Bodie's "This game is rigged, man." when Count day fails, Prop Joe's "Look the part, be the part.", Avon's "I'm just a gangster, I suppose." and Marlo's "My name is my name!" at the top two ranks, and Omar's "A man got to have a code.", "All in the game, yo." and "Oh, indeed." when he robs you. Run Omar over and, when he gets back up, you'll hear the one about coming at the king. He also stops waiting to come after you.
  • Locust Point payphone: Nick Sobotka (The package), Ziggy (Clean wheels). The Pit: Bodie (Count day), D'Angelo (Re-up round). Union hall: Frank Sobotka (The can), Horseface (Off the manifest). Detail office: Lester Freamon (The tail), McNulty (Marine unit). City desk: Gus Haynes (Deadline), Mr. Prezbo (School run), Senator Davis (The contribution). Appliance shop, East Baltimore (new sixth phone): Proposition Joe (The co-op, Not a toaster).
  • Omar walks the Westside, the Eastside and Hamsterdam in a long coat. You hear him first: a whistled nursery tune. Other people run. If you're on foot with $50 or more he comes for you and takes 15% of your cash (max $250), then leaves you alone for a while. Get in a car, or step into the free zone, and he lets you be.
  • Bubbles pushes his cart around the same neighborhoods selling white tees for $20 (white ring that moves with him, E on foot). If you're wanted, he sends the police the wrong way: one star off.
  • Cutty trains you at the boxing gym ($ marker just below it): $300 once, permanently faster sprint.
  • Police radio names who's after you as the stars climb (Herc and Carver, Kima, Daniels, Rawls), Landsman signs you out when you're busted, and Bodie, Stringer, Avon and Marlo each have something to say as you rank up.

Work: five phones, five institutions

  • Walk or drive into a pink ring. Each phone hands out its own jobs, alternating between two. Pay rises and clocks tighten as your rank goes up.
  • Payphone, Locust Point (where you start): The package (pick up and deliver by car) and Clean wheels (boost the gold coupe, 2 stars, deliver it undamaged to the garage).
  • Payphone in the Pit: Count day (three collections across the city) and Re-up round (checkpoint sprint around the Westside grid).
  • Union hall at the terminal: The can (drive a slow gold van across town, 1 star, damage costs you) and Off the manifest (fish three floating boxes out of the harbor by boat, back to the terminal pier).
  • Detail office: The tail (stay on a target through nine corners) and Marine unit (boat out to Fort Carroll, recover the evidence bag on foot, bring it back). Police work: no wanted level.
  • City desk, downtown: Deadline (three scenes before the paper closes) and School run (collect the kids at the middle school, stop at each home).
  • Hacking: steal a taxi with no job and no wanted level and fares start automatically. $100 bonus every fifth ride in a row.
  • Ranks: New in town, Known on the corner (2 jobs), Earner (5), Lieutenant (9), Runs the Westside (14).

Police and the free zone

  • Stars rise for carjacking in view of police (or when someone calls it in), stealing a cruiser, knocking people over and ramming police. More stars, more and faster cruisers; harbor police boats at 2+ stars on the water.
  • Busted = stopped or pinned next to police for about a second. Lose them by distance, or at a green spray shop (downtown and on the Westside, $100, also repairs).
  • Hamsterdam is a free zone: inside the red dashed line nobody busts you, and your wanted level fades even with cruisers right behind you.
  • Wasted sends you to the nearest hospital, busted to the nearest station, up to $100 either way.

The harbor

  • Boats: the wooden boat and a sailboat at the Locust Point pier, a jet ski at Fort Carroll, another boat at the terminal pier. The sailboat needs the wind (watch the arrow; you can't sail straight into it).
  • Crabbing: stop on a crab pot buoy (ripples and gulls) until the bar fills. Hold of 6, from a few small crabs ($15) to a trophy rockfish ($300). Slow down at either pier and the crab house buys the lot.
  • Harbor run: the blue GO ring off the Locust Point pier starts a timed buoy course out around the lighthouse. $120 to finish, $300 for a personal best.
  • Floating stars on the water are salvage, worth $10 each by boat.

Money and health

  • Earn from jobs, hack rides, crabbing, the harbor run, cash dropped by pedestrians, coins ($5) and gems ($25) around the city, and the parking challenge in the Locust Point lay-by.
  • Spend at the white $ markers on foot: speedboat $2,000 by the pier, Coupe GT on your driveway $4,000.
  • Carry-outs (orange rings: Locust Point waterfront, the Pit, East Baltimore): a chicken box for $10 restores most of your health. Health also regenerates slowly when you're not wanted.
  • Progress saves in this browser, separately from the Seaside version.

Radio

  • R cycles: Westside Soul (lo-fi jazz chords, the default), Pratt Street Blues (an original 12-bar shuffle with a harmonica-style lead), Charm City Club (130 bpm club breaks and brass chops), Harbor Rock 101, Night Shift FM (synthwave), Island Breeze (reggae), Radio off. All generated live, no audio files.

Cheats

  • Type while playing: CASH, NOCOPS, FIXIT.

About

  • An unofficial, non-commercial fan homage built on real Baltimore geography and the settings and characters of the series, not affiliated with its makers. Character names and a handful of short, attributed signature lines appear as a tribute; all other dialogue is original, no actor is depicted, and no music or footage from the show is used. The whistled tune is a traditional nursery rhyme in the public domain.
